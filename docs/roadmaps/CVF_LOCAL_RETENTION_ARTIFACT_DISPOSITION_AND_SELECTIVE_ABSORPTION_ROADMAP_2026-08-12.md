authority, creating a second CVF foundation, or retaining indefinite local
clutter. Absorption is selective and source-verified; rejection, supersession,
and evidence-only retention are valid terminal results.

- no runtime, daemon, watcher, workspace MCP, IDE bridge, Web, or UI build;
- no provider/live execution, secrets, network, public-sync, push, or deploy;
- no deletion or mutation of the retention ZIP during T0;
- no claim that generated bytecode, corrupt event copies, or raw snapshots are
