## Purpose

Define the governed systemization route that turns the accepted MinerU private
smoke receipt policy into a practical CVF workflow-chain plan. The route keeps
the high-value document-layer capability moving while preventing a premature
use-case/product detour or a production claim.

  planning boundary.
