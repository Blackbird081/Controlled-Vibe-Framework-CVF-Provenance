### Learning-Curve Null Hypothesis

The null remains live: P3-A was a first large Windows tranche and may contain
one-time discovery cost. The cross-sectional P2-C/P2-D comparison shows P3-A is
anomalous but does not show whether the next comparable tranche naturally
declines. There is no untreated repeated P3-A-like comparator. Full-program
causal claims must therefore be deferred.
